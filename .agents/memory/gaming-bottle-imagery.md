name: Gaming bottle imagery
description: Visual fidelity and packaging disclosure for the Chicago gaming collection
---

For Chicago gaming fragrance imagery, use faithful crops or composites of the supplied bottle photos rather than asking an image model to redraw the labels. Keep the reference-bottle disclosure prominent whenever those images are used to sell decants.

**Why:** Image models can distort the distinctive label lettering, and the customer receives a different decant bottle rather than the pictured packaging.

**How to apply:** When refreshing product images, preserve the photographed bottle and lettering, polish the lighting/background around them, and check that product listings and checkout still distinguish the reference image from shipped packaging.

TDG original-bottle search results can be misleading: product titles may describe a full bottle while the image shows only a travel decant or a box. Baccarat Rouge 540 and its Extrait must also have distinct reference photos.

**Why:** Multiple search refinements were necessary to find full bottles for Cactus Garden and Gucci Oud rather than sample packaging.

**How to apply:** Visually inspect downloaded reference photos, not just search titles. Keep source provenance, verify the exact scent and variant, and distinguish the designer reference bottle from the TDG decant being sold. Publicly accessible photos are not automatically licensed for storefront use.
