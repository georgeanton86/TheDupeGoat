---
name: Trial purchase promotions
description: Owner's intended lifespan for temporary checkout discounts
---

The temporary purchase promotions are for the owner's trial purchase: “I'm going to use it one time for the purchase then we can shut it off.”

**Why:** The user wants to verify a real purchase without paying full price, not launch a standing public promotion.

**How to apply:** Disable the temporary codes after the owner confirms the purchase is finished. Do not mistake a cart preview or test run for that real purchase.
