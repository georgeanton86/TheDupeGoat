The temporary purchase promotions are for the owner's trial purchase: “I'm going to use it one time for the purchase then we can shut it off.”

**Why:** The user wants to verify a real purchase without paying full price, not launch a standing public promotion.

**How to apply:** The owner confirmed the real trial purchase worked and asked to delete both codes. Keep those temporary promotions disabled unless explicitly requested again. Do not mistake a cart preview or test run for a real purchase.
